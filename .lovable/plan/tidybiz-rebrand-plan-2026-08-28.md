# TidyBiz Rebrand Plan

## Goal
Rebrand the existing NigeriaCAC website to **TidyBiz**, shifting from a pure CAC-registration positioning to a dual-pillar startup launchpad: **Company Registration** + **Brand Identity**. The chosen visual direction is the "Precision dual-pillar hero" with frosted-glass service cards, an indigo/slate palette, and Plus Jakarta Sans typography.

## Scope of Work

### 1. Global Brand & Styling
- Replace all instances of `NigeriaCAC` with `TidyBiz` across copy, email, footer, and metadata.
- Update `index.html`: title becomes "TidyBiz — Company Registration & Brand Identity for Nigerian Startups"; description and Open Graph tags updated to match.
- Replace the green/emerald color scheme with an indigo/slate palette:
  - Primary brand color: indigo-600
  - Background: slate-50
  - Cards: white with slate-200 borders and frosted-glass hover states
  - Dark accents: slate-900
- Load Plus Jakarta Sans via Google Fonts and apply it as the site typeface.

### 2. Header
- Update logo to a simple geometric mark + "TidyBiz" wordmark.
- Navigation links: Registration, Branding, Process, Testimonials, Contact.
- CTA button: "Get Started".

### 3. Hero — Precision Dual-Pillar
- Centered headline: "From Idea to Official." with "Official" in indigo.
- Subheadline positions TidyBiz as the all-in-one Nigerian startup launchpad.
- Two prominent frosted-glass cards below the headline:
  - **Pillar 01 — Company Registration**: CAC business name, LTD/LLP, TIN, tax docs.
  - **Pillar 02 — Brand Identity**: logo, brand guidelines, socials setup, website.
- Each card has a hover lift/border glow, icon, feature bullets, and a primary CTA.

### 4. Services Section
- Split the existing 6-card grid into two clearly labeled groups:
  - **Register**: Business Name, Limited Company, Trademark, Partnership, Express, Post-Incorporation.
  - **Brand**: Logo & Visual Identity, Brand Guidelines, Social Media Setup, Website/Landing Page, Pitch Deck Design, Brand Photography Direction.
- Keep Naira pricing; update descriptions to match TidyBiz voice.

### 5. Process Section
- Retain the 4-step structure but reframe copy around the combined CAC + brand journey.
- Update step titles/descriptions to mention both legal filing and brand delivery milestones.

### 6. Testimonials
- Rewrite all testimonials with Nigerian names, companies, and contexts.
- Mention both CAC registration and branding/website outcomes where natural.

### 7. Contact Section
- Update headings and copy to reflect the dual-pillar offering.
- Update service select options to include branding services.
- Update contact email to a TidyBiz address.

### 8. Footer
- Replace NigeriaCAC footer with TidyBiz branding and links.
- Group links under Register, Brand, Company, Contact.
- Update copyright and contact details.

## Outcome
A fully rebranded single-page React/Tailwind site that reads as TidyBiz, visually communicates two equal service pillars, and uses the approved indigo/slate frosted-glass aesthetic.
